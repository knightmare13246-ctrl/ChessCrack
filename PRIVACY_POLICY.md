# Privacy Policy for ChessCrack

**Last updated: September 2026**

**ChessCrack** ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user data.

---

### 1. Zero Data Collection
ChessCrack is designed from the ground up to respect your privacy.
- **No Personal Information Collected**: We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, physical location, or device identifiers.
- **No Analytics / Telemetry**: ChessCrack contains **zero analytics SDKs, zero crash-reporting trackers, and zero third-party telemetry tools**.
- **No Advertisements**: The application is 100% ad-free and contains no advertising SDKs or tracking cookies.

---

### 2. On-Device Local Processing
All chess engine computations, evaluations, and neural network inferences performed by **Stockfish** and **Leela Chess Zero (Lc0)** are executed **entirely locally on your device**.
- No chess positions, moves, or game analysis are transmitted over the internet.
- Saved games, variation trees, and settings are stored strictly in local application storage on your device and are never synchronized to external servers.

---

### 3. Network Permissions
The app requests standard network permissions solely to support downloading optional engine weights, themes, or opening external links you deliberately click (such as GitHub release pages or source documentation). The app does not transmit background telemetry.
