================================================================================
HOW TO INSTALL CHESSCRACK ON YOUR ANDROID DEVICE
================================================================================

ChessCrack is packaged as a standard Android application package (.apk).
You do not need a computer, Android Studio, ADB, or developer mode to install it.

Follow these simple steps:

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
METHOD 1: DIRECT DOWNLOAD / SHARING VIA CHAT OR CLOUD (RECOMMENDED)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Send the file `ChessCrack-v1.0.0.apk` (or `ChessCrack-release.apk`) to your phone
   via WhatsApp, Telegram, Google Drive, Bluetooth, or email.

2. On your Android phone, open your Downloads folder or tap the received file.

3. If prompted with "For your security, your phone is not allowed to install
   unknown apps from this source":
   a. Tap "Settings" in the dialog.
   b. Turn ON "Allow from this source" (or "Install unknown apps").
   c. Tap Back to return to the installer.

4. Tap "Install".

5. When installation completes, tap "Open" or launch ChessCrack from your home screen.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
METHOD 2: USB TRANSFER FROM COMPUTER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Connect your Android phone to your PC via a USB cable.
2. On your phone, set the USB mode to "File Transfer" (MTP).
3. On your PC, copy `release/ChessCrack-v1.0.0.apk` to your phone's "Download" folder.
4. On your phone, open the "Files" or "My Files" app, navigate to "Downloads", and tap `ChessCrack-v1.0.0.apk`.
5. Tap "Install" and grant permission if prompted.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
METHOD 3: INSTALL VIA ADB (FOR ADVANCED USERS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

If your device is connected via USB with USB Debugging enabled:

   adb install -r release/ChessCrack-v1.0.0.apk

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FIRST LAUNCH & ENGINE ACTIVATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Open ChessCrack.
2. The default engine (Stockfish) initializes automatically with high-performance
   native evaluation.
3. To switch between Stockfish and Leela Chess Zero (Lc0):
   - Tap the Engine dropdown in the header or analysis drawer.
   - Select either "Stockfish" or "Lc0".
4. To customize sound effects and appearance:
   - Tap the Settings gear icon in the top header.
   - Adjust Move Sounds (ON/OFF), volume slider, sound theme, and board style.
